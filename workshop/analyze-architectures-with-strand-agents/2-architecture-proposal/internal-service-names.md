# Internal Service Names

The list of internal service names are the second level headings below.  The description for each service name is the paragraph content until the next header.

## Shakespeare

The editorial service where textbooks are authored.

## Gutenberg

The publishing service that converts textbooks into output formats likes PDF or ePub.

## Alexandria

The library service where published textbooks are stored.

## Hemingway

The content review and approval service for editorial workflows.

## Dickens

The collaborative editing and version control service.

## Orwell

The content moderation and compliance service.

## Chaucer

The content versioning and history service.

## Blake

The image and media processing service.
